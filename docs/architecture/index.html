Product Identity & Overview

iDeal Backend — Project Overview #

Product Identity #

  • Name: iDeal (previously referenced as Makon in early prototypes)
  • Domain: Property management platform for rental properties in Uzbekistan
  • Primary Market: Tashkent (Toshkent) and surrounding districts
  • Business Model: Trust management (Mulkni Ishonchli Boshqarish) — the platform guarantees a fixed monthly income to property owners while managing tenants, maintenance, and compliance.

What Problem It Solves #

For Property Owners #

  • Problem: Finding reliable tenants, dealing with vacancies, handling repairs, chasing rent payments, and navigating informal/unregistered rental arrangements.
  • Solution: iDeal signs a formal trust-management contract with the owner, pays them a guaranteed monthly rate regardless of whether the unit is occupied, and handles all tenant-facing operations.

For Tenants #

  • Problem: Finding verified, clean, legally documented apartments with responsive maintenance and modern payment options.
  • Solution: iDeal offers vetted listings with official contracts, 24/7 maintenance support, and transparent pricing.

For the Platform (Management) #

  • Revenue Model: The spread between the tenant's monthly rent and the owner's guaranteed payout.
  • Example: Owner market rate = $500. Platform guarantees owner $380. Platform charges tenant $500. Monthly margin = $120.
  • Additional Revenue: Agent commissions on new lease deals.

User Roles & Responsibilities #

1. Management (Platform Admin) #

  • Full access to the entire portfolio
  • Add properties, manage owner contracts, manage tenant leases
  • View P&L (Profit & Loss), occupancy, vacancy cost
  • Process payments manually (mark tenant payments as received, schedule owner payouts)
  • Manage maintenance requests and assign technicians
  • Manage agent roster and commission tracking
  • View lease expiry calendars and trigger renewals
  • Publish listings to the marketplace

2. Owner (Property Owner) #

  • Views their own properties and their guaranteed monthly income
  • Views payment history (guaranteed payouts)
  • Understands why their guaranteed rate is lower than market rate (vacancy risk coverage, maintenance, international tenants, legal protection)
  • Cannot modify properties or see other owners' data

3. Tenant (Renter) #

  • Views their current apartment details, lease end date, and rent amount
  • Pays rent (initially: manually recorded by admin; future: integrated payment gateways)
  • Submits maintenance requests with priority levels
  • Browses other available listings in the marketplace

4. Marketplace (Public / Unauthenticated) #

  • Browse available (vacant) listings
  • Filter by district, price, rooms, area
  • View listings on a map
  • Book a viewing appointment
  • No authentication required for browsing

Core Business Process Flow #

Step 1: Property Onboarding #

  1. Management identifies a property or an owner approaches iDeal.
  2. Property is added to the system with location details (district, address, floor, rooms, area).
  3. Pricing is negotiated:
  • Owner's asking price (market rate on OLX, etc.) = ask_price
  • iDeal's offer to owner (guaranteed, typically ~24% below ask) = owner_guaranteed_price
  • iDeal's charge to tenant (market rate) = tenant_charge_price
  1. Owner Agreement is created (12/24/36 months, notarized contract).
  2. Listing is automatically published to the marketplace (OLX, Joymee, Bir-Bir — via future integrations or manual posting).

Step 2: Tenant Acquisition #

  1. Tenant finds listing via marketplace or agent.
  2. Lease is signed (start date, end date, monthly rent = tenant_charge_price).
  3. Property status changes to rented.
  4. Tenant moves in; property score may be updated based on condition.

Step 3: Monthly Operations #

  1. Tenant pays rent (manually recorded by admin for now; later via Click/Payme/Uzcard).
  2. Payment record is created: PaymentType.TENANT_RENT, status paid.
  3. On the 25th of each month, admin schedules or records owner payout:
  • PaymentType.OWNER_PAYOUT, status paid or pending.
  1. Maintenance requests may be submitted by tenant → assigned to technician → resolved.

Step 4: Lease Renewal or Vacancy #

  1. As lease end date approaches (e.g., < 60 days), system alerts management.
  2. Management contacts tenant for renewal.
  3. If renewed: new lease record is created; old lease is archived.
  4. If not renewed: property status changes to vacant; listing is republished.
  5. Vacancy cost is tracked: vacant_days * daily_rent = lost revenue.

Step 5: Financial Reporting #

  1. P&L Dashboard shows monthly gross revenue, owner payouts, net margin, and tax liability.
  2. Tax declarations are generated from payment records (4% turnover tax on gross rental income).
  3. All transactions are bank-transfer-based for traceability.

Key Metrics Tracked #

Metric Description
Occupancy Rate Rented properties / Total properties
Monthly Gross Revenue Sum of all tenant rents collected
Monthly Net Margin Gross revenue − Owner payouts
Vacancy Cost Days vacant × daily rent for each vacant property
Owner Guaranteed Yield Guaranteed payout / Market asking price
Tenant Satisfaction Score Property-level rating (1–10)
Agent Performance Deals closed, total revenue generated
Maintenance Resolution Time Average time from open to resolved
Payment On-Time Rate Percentage of tenant payments received on or before due date

Currency Strategy #

  • Each property/deal stores its preferred currency (USD or UZS) and the amount in that currency.
  • The system supports dynamic conversion between USD and UZS using an exchange rate service or stored daily rate.
  • Tax reporting and local compliance are calculated in UZS after conversion.
  • Financial dashboards may display both currencies where relevant.

Out of Scope (Initial Phase) #

The following are not part of the initial backend design and will be deferred to future iterations:

  • Tax Inspector role / dashboard: Deferred. Compliance data is still generated internally, but no dedicated inspector UI or API.
  • Automated payment gateways: Tenant payments are manually recorded by admin. Click, Payme, Uzcard/Humo integrations are planned for v2.
  • OLX/Joymee/Bir-Bir auto-publishing: Manual listing for now; API integrations for auto-posting are future work.
  • Telegram bot notifications: Noted as a useful feature for the UZ market but deferred.
  • AI/agent recommendations: Not in scope.

Backend Module Structure (Django Apps) #

All apps live under src/apps/.

account (Existing) #

  • Responsibility: User authentication, registration, roles, profiles.
  • Key Models: User (custom, extended from AbstractBaseUser)
  • Extensions Needed: role, is_verified, phone, telegram_id, nationality

core (Existing) #

  • Responsibility: Base abstractions, utilities, middleware, shared helpers.
  • Key Models: TimestampedModel, SoftDeleteModel, BaseManager
  • Key Utilities: Pagination, logging (Telegram error alerts), request context filter

property (New) #

  • Responsibility: Physical properties, districts, photos, amenities, status tracking.
  • Key Models: Property, District, PropertyPhoto, PropertyAmenity

contract (New) #

  • Responsibility: Legal agreements between owner and platform, and between platform and tenant.
  • Key Models: OwnerAgreement, Lease, LeaseRenewal

finance (New) #

  • Responsibility: All monetary transactions: tenant rent collection, owner payouts, agent commissions.
  • Key Models: Payment, ExchangeRate, PayoutSchedule
  • Note: Payments are manually entered by admin in v1. No automated payment gateways yet.

maintenance (New) #

  • Responsibility: Service requests from tenants, technician assignments, resolution tracking.
  • Key Models: ServiceRequest, ServiceRequestAssignment, MaintenanceLog

marketplace (New) #

  • Responsibility: Public-facing listing data, search/filter APIs, booking/viewing requests.
  • Key Models: Listing, ViewingRequest
  • Note: This is a read-heavy module; may benefit from caching and denormalized listing views.

agent (New) #

  • Responsibility: Commission-based broker/agent management, deal tracking.
  • Key Models: Agent, AgentDeal, CommissionRule

tax (New — Deferred) #

  • Responsibility: Internal tax declaration generation, compliance reports.
  • Key Models: Declaration (generated monthly from finance data)
  • Note: No dedicated Tax Inspector API or UI in v1. Data is generated for internal/management use.

API Communication Contract #

Frontend ↔ Backend #

  • Protocol: HTTPS only
  • Format: JSON (via DMR's JsonParser/JsonRenderer)
  • Authentication: JWT Bearer token in Authorization header
  • CORS: Enabled for /api/* endpoints only
  • Base URL: /api/v1/

JWT Token Strategy #

  • Access Token: 1 hour lifetime (HS256)
  • Refresh Token: 30 days lifetime
  • Issuer: iDeal
  • Endpoints:
  • POST /api/v1/token/ — Obtain access + refresh
  • POST /api/v1/token/refresh/ — Refresh access token

API Design Principles #

  • Role-based access control (RBAC): Every endpoint checks the user's role before proceeding.
  • Soft deletes: All business entities use soft-delete (via django-soft-delete) for audit trails.
  • Timestamped: Every entity tracks created_at and updated_at.
  • Pagination: List endpoints use standardized pagination (cursor or offset-based).
  • OpenAPI: Auto-generated schema via DMR's OpenAPIConfig for frontend type generation.

Background Jobs (Django-Q2) #

Scheduled and async tasks running via Redis-backed Django-Q2 cluster:

Task Frequency Description
lease_expiry_alert Daily Find leases expiring in ≤60 days; notify management
owner_payout_schedule Monthly (25th) Generate pending OWNER_PAYOUT payment records
vacancy_cost_update Daily Recalculate vacant_days and lost_revenue for vacant properties
tax_declaration_generate Monthly (1st) Generate tax.Declaration from prior month's gross revenue
listing_sync Daily Refresh marketplace listing availability status

Deployment & Environment #

Development #

  • uv sync → python manage.py migrate → python manage.py runserver
  • Docker Compose: docker-compose up --build

Production #

  • docker-compose -f docker-compose.prod.yml up --build
  • WhiteNoise for static files
  • PostgreSQL with connection pooling (conn_max_age=600)
  • Redis for cache and Q2 queue
  • Sentry SDK for error tracking

Security Considerations #

  • Password hashing: PBKDF2 SHA256 (Django default)
  • CSRF: Disabled for API endpoints; JWT handles stateless auth
  • SQL Injection: Protected by Django ORM
  • XSS: Protected by DMR's JSON renderer (no HTML rendering)
  • Audit: Soft deletes + timestamped models preserve history
  • Logging: All 500 errors are logged to console + Telegram (if configured)
  • Media uploads: Restricted file types and sizes; stored outside web root

Scalability Notes #

  • The modular app structure allows each domain to be split into a separate service later if needed.
  • marketplace is the most read-heavy module; it can be denormalized or cached aggressively.
  • finance is write-heavy and audit-sensitive; it should remain in the primary database with strong indexing.
  • Redis is already provisioned for caching and task queues; can be expanded for rate limiting and session storage.

Data Models & Storage

iDeal Backend — Data Models & Database Design #

Conventions #

  • Primary Key: All tables use BigAutoField (Django default).
  • Soft Deletes: All business entities inherit from SoftDeleteModel (via django-soft-delete).
  • Timestamps: All entities inherit from TimestampedModel (created_at, updated_at).
  • Currency: Amounts stored as DecimalField(max_digits=12, decimal_places=2) with an associated currency field (USD or UZS).
  • Exchange Rates: A separate ExchangeRate table stores daily rates for dynamic conversion.

Currency & Conversion Rules #

  1. Storage: Every monetary field is stored with its original currency (USD or UZS).
  2. Preferred Currency: The Property table defines the preferred currency for that property's pricing fields (ask_price, owner_guaranteed_price, tenant_charge_price).
  3. Conversion: When a dashboard or report needs a unified currency, the system:
  • Looks up the latest ExchangeRate for the date in question.
  • Converts on-the-fly using Decimal arithmetic for precision.
  • Never mutates the stored original amount.
  1. Tax: Declaration stores both the original currency tax and the UZS equivalent for local compliance.

WebSocket Realtime Protocol

Chat realtime protocol #

Chat clients connect to GET /ws/v1/chat/ using ws:// locally and wss://

in production. The upgrade request accepts the existing API access token in the

Authorization: Bearer header. Browser clients may instead use

the ideal_access cookie. The server closes unauthenticated connections with

code 4401.

The endpoint is deliberately an event stream, not a second write API. Clients

continue to use the documented REST endpoints for sending messages and then

refresh their authoritative state when an event arrives. This keeps retries,

idempotency (client_id), validation and error envelopes consistent across

web and mobile.

Frames #

After a successful connection the server sends:

json
{"type":"chat.ready","latest_event_id":184,"heartbeat_seconds":25}

Clients persist the largest event_id, ignore duplicate or older events, and

immediately send a sync request:

json
{"type":"chat.sync","after_event_id":184}

If an event cursor has expired, the server emits chat.resync_required. The

client must refresh chat summaries and the active conversation via REST, reset

its cursor, then send chat.sync again. It must not fill the gap with polling.

Send {"type":"chat.ping"} at least every 25 seconds while connected.

The durable event types are chat.message.created, chat.conversation.updated,

chat.conversation.removed, and chat.read.updated. They include an

event_id, conversation_id, conversation_version, and the corresponding

message or conversation payload. A client should fetch messages after its

known message ID and refresh the conversation summary rather than assuming an

event is a complete local snapshot.

Typing is transient and opt-in. Send

{"type":"chat.typing.set","conversation_id":42,"is_typing":true} and stop

within five seconds; the server broadcasts chat.typing.updated only to the

other permitted participant. Typing frames are never persisted or replayed.

Operations and deployment #

The ASGI server must serve both HTTP and WebSocket traffic, and all instances

must use the same Redis deployment for CHANNEL_LAYERS and task scheduling.

The proxy/load balancer must forward WebSocket upgrades and preserve the

Authorization header or the ideal_access cookie. Configure its idle timeout

above 30 seconds; 60 seconds is the application channel expiry default.

Required settings are REDIS_URL, optional CHAT_CHANNEL_CAPACITY (default

200), CHAT_CHANNEL_EXPIRY_SECONDS (default 60),

CHAT_REALTIME_EVENT_RETENTION_DAYS (default 7), and

CHAT_REALTIME_REPLAY_LIMIT (default 500). The daily Django-Q task

prune-chat-realtime-events removes expired durable events. Monitor socket

connects, reconnects, close codes, Redis channel errors, event backlog, and

REST refresh failures; alert on sustained replay/resync or authentication

close-code spikes.

Read receipts are visibility-based: clients send the highest incoming message

that has been at least 60% visible while the app is foregrounded. The server

clamps that watermark to an existing message, recomputes unread counts, and

broadcasts the changed receipt. Sending and read state are represented; there

is no delivered state.

Background Tasks & Cron Jobs

Background Tasks (Django-Q2) #

This project uses Django-Q2 for background task management.

Defining Tasks #

You can queue any regular Python function as a background task. It's recommended to place them in a tasks.py file within your app.

python
def process_data(data_id: int):
    # Perform background processing
    print(f"Processing {data_id}...")
    return True

Initiating a Task #

To properly initiate a background task, use async_task from django_q.tasks and pass the function along with its arguments. This sends the task to the queue.

python
from django_q.tasks import async_task
from myapp.tasks import process_data

# Queue the task
task_id = async_task(process_data, 123)

# For scheduled tasks, you can use the Schedule model or schedule() function
# from django_q.tasks import schedule
# schedule(process_data, 123, hook='myapp.hooks.print_result', schedule_type='O')

Running the Cluster #

When using Docker, the qcluster starts automatically. To run it locally:

bash
python manage.py qcluster

Configuration #

The cluster configuration is located in src/config/settings/base.py under the Q_CLUSTER dictionary. It uses Redis as a broker, configured via the REDIS_URL environment variable.

Further Reading #

For advanced configuration, scheduled tasks, and cluster monitoring, refer to the official Django-Q2 documentation.

Architecture rules now enforced #

  • Pydantic success/error envelopes and typed pagination are the core response

primitives.

  • A controller may construct services only through the per-request factory.
  • A migrated query model parses known keys; a PydanticFilterSet applies only

filtering/search/order to a service-prepared queryset.

  • Generic pagination slices before Pydantic output conversion.
  • One-off action URLs are POST-only; the ratchet test verifies the controller

method set and HTTP 405 responses.

  • New architecture tests deliberately cover only migrated slices. The test

suite must grow the ratchet as each bounded context migrates rather than

falsely allowing the pre-existing repository-wide violations.

Required gates before declaring the refactor complete #

  1. Migrate identity/communication, property lifecycle, and operations/finance

domains (including each public, mobile, and management controller).

  1. Replace every remaining output helper, raw response dictionary, broad catch,

direct controller ORM workflow, static-only service, and API cross-import.

  1. Move cross-aggregate model/signal behavior into application services and

remove business-critical signals only after all callers are migrated.

  1. Add resolver-wide method manifest, per-endpoint response/OpenAPI parity,

full filter matrices, service transaction/idempotency tests, and query-count

budgets.

  1. Enable DMR response validation in development/tests, regenerate/validate

Bruno and browser documentation after each route slice, and run final system

checks, Ruff, migrations, coverage, and full tests. Existing non-mobile

integration baseline failures must be reported separately if still present.
